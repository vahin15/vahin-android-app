# IARC Content Rating Questionnaire Guide (Unifest)

When submitting Unifest to the Google Play Console, you will complete the **IARC (International Age Rating Coalition)** questionnaire. Use the following answers:

---

## 1. Category
- **App Category:** `Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other` (Select **Communication** or **Social**)

---

## 2. Content Questions

### **Violence / Fear / Sexual Content / Drugs / Gambling**
- Does the app contain violence? 👉 **No**
- Does the app contain fear/horror themes? 👉 **No**
- Does the app contain sexuality or nudity? 👉 **No**
- Does the app contain references to drugs, alcohol, or tobacco? 👉 **No**
- Does the app contain simulated gambling? 👉 **No**

### **User Interaction (Crucial for Play Store Compliance)**
- **Does the app natively allow users to interact or exchange content with other users through voice, text, or sharing photos/files?**  
  👉 **Yes**
- **Does the app share the user's current and precise physical location with other users?**  
  👉 **No**
- **Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods?**  
  👉 **No** (unless in-app purchases are enabled later)

---

## 3. Expected Rating
- With "Users Interact" and "Unrestricted Internet / Communication" declared, the app will typically receive a **Teen / 12+** or **Everyone 10+** rating depending on territory (due to open P2P communication between arbitrary users).
- Because Unifest includes User Blocking (`blockUser`) and User Reporting (`reportUser`) in compliance with Google Play's User Generated Content (UGC) policy, the app meets all requirements for communication apps.
