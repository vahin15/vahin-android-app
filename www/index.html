🛡️ Privacy Policy
P2P Encrypted: Unifest does not store your private text messages, voice calls, video streams, or shared files on central servers. Data is transferred directly between devices using encrypted WebRTC.
1. Information We Collect
• Phone Number & ID: Used solely for user discovery and routing.
• Push Token (FCM): Used by Firebase to wake your phone for incoming calls and messages.
• Presence: Real-time online/offline status for peer connectivity.
• Advertising ID: Collected by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner ads.
• Diagnostics: Anonymous crash logs collected via Firebase Crashlytics.
2. What We DO NOT Collect
• No chat message logs or voice/video recordings on central servers.
• No contact book harvesting or selling of personal information.
3. WebRTC Relays (TURN)
When direct peer connection is blocked by restrictive firewalls, encrypted packets are relayed through standard TURN servers. Relayed media cannot be decrypted or stored.
4. Account & Data Deletion
You can erase all your account data at any time via Settings > Account > Delete Account & Data or online via the public Data Deletion Form.
5. Developer Contact
Email: unifest.messenger@gmail.com
