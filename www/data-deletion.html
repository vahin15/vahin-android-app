In-App Deletion Available: If you currently have the app installed, you can delete your account instantly by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account & Data inside the app.

What Data is Deleted?

Submitting an account deletion request permanently removes:

  • Your registered Unifest User ID / Phone Number from signaling records
  • Your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification registration token
  • Any pending offline signaling mailboxes or notification queues

Note: Unifest does not store your chat messages or call logs on central servers (communications are peer-to-peer and stored only on your personal device).
